## Report structure — Consumer · Premium

_Informational document. Extended public references + factual timeline. No prognosis._

### Anatomy

1. **Header**
2. **Practical results & statutory damages** (catalogued ranges only)
3. **Case under review** — facts from the form
4. **Factual timeline of cited precedents**
5. **What U.S. law says** (plain language)
6. **Precedents (extended set)**
7. **Comparative notes** (Premium — observed requirements only)
8. **Sample count** (when available)
9. **Transparency close**

### Rules

- Do not invent case citations, amounts, or URLs.
- Statutory damages appear only from the curated reference file — never invented averages.
- Timeline describes cited public cases only — not advice for the requester.

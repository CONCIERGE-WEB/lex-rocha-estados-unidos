## Report structure — Consumer · Essential

_Informational document. Up to 2 public references. No prognosis._

### Anatomy

1. **Header**
2. **Practical results & statutory damages** (catalogued ranges only)
3. **Case under review** — facts from the form
4. **Precedents (up to 2)**
5. **Sample count** (when available)
6. **Transparency close**

### Rules

- Do not invent case citations, amounts, or URLs.
- Statutory damages appear only from the curated reference file — never invented averages.
